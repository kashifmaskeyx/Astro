Picture a pizza. The crust, the sauce, the cheese, that's your static HTML. It's done before anyone orders.

## The toppings are the islands

Only the toppings that need to be fresh, like the basil, get added at the last second. In Astro, those are your interactive components.

Everything else was already baked.
