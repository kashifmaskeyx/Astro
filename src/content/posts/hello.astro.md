I used to ship a whole app to show a blog post. It felt wrong, and it was slow.

## What changed

Astro builds the page ahead of time and sends plain HTML. The browser has less to do, so the page shows up faster.

## What stayed the same

I still write components. I still use TypeScript. I still reach for React when I need it.
